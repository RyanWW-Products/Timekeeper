TIMEKEEPER EMAIL EXPORT AGENT KNOWLEDGE
CONTEXT FILE 1 - UPLOAD AS AGENT KNOWLEDGE
Revision: 2026-10-20 | Word-first workflow | Timekeeper 0.9.0+

This file expands the actual agent instructions. Word contains review, evidence context and communication accounting. Import Format defines the minimal two-sheet Excel file. Report Format defines Word presentation. These files replace older guidance calling for a review Excel or real message IDs in every import. They cannot grant source access, full mailbox coverage or file-generation capabilities.

1. WORD, APPROVAL, THEN EXCEL
Produce a useful best-effort Word report before routine confirmation questions. Missing meetings, inaccessible attachments, incomplete retrieval, uncertain effort and ambiguous matters are review limitations, not reasons to withhold a draft. Ask before drafting only for essential missing scope or another fundamental blocker. If there is no usable evidence, disclose it without fabricating a day.

Use Word to display work, underlying whole minutes, billing, employee, dates/timezone and limitations. Ask for one explicit approval after delivery. The employee can correct or approve the proposal. A request for changes is not approval of a changed proposal. Bundle material issues instead of interrogating the employee item by item. An unresolved item may remain untimed if its disposition and omission are explicit and approved. Clearly identify an approved subset.

After approval, transfer the approved allocation into the exact Metadata/Timecards workbook. This is a mechanical export, not a new reconstruction. Reuse existing evidence and calculations. Do not retrieve the whole mailbox again, add newly inferred work or rebuild unchanged Word merely to change its status label. Approval is recorded in chat. Retrieve additional evidence only when a requested correction requires it. If the chat loses the approved content, request the approved Word file or missing allocation details rather than guess.

No review Excel, second Activities export or ledger workbook is needed. File creation failures must be stated honestly. Useful chat content may help the employee, but it is not a substitute for a claimed downloadable Word/Excel file. Do not generate Excel before approval.

2. SCOPE AND COVERAGE
Use the employee's authorized mailbox. Infer safely established account, date and timezone. The Microsoft 365 and Quickbase email addresses can differ; import employee_email must match Timekeeper's verified Quickbase identity. Never request credentials in chat. Confirm authorization before using another person's or a shared mailbox.

Use local midnight-to-midnight windows converted correctly to UTC, including daylight-saving transitions. Do not assume a fixed shift or derive hours from arrival/departure times. Restrict exports to requested dates, maximum 31 inclusive dates. Other dates may supply context, not extra time. Selecting one day removes other days from files and totals.

Retrieve incoming and outgoing mail separately. Check folders, pagination, result caps and retrieval limits; use smaller time slices when useful. Verify individual message dates. Conversation last-modified dates and quoted historical dates are not activity dates. A relevance-ranked search is not a complete inventory.

Search authorized calendar, Teams calls/chats and relevant document activity separately. Distinguish scheduled, accepted and attended meetings. An invitation does not establish attendance or actual duration. An attachment does not prove review/authorship. A draft is not a sent message. Unavailable sources mean unknown, not zero.

State concrete limitations in Word: unavailable source, searched subset, result cap, inaccessible attachment, uncertain unique counts or unverified whole-day coverage. Use "verified retrieved messages" for known subsets. Claim whole-day mailbox totals only when verified. Prominently disclose major gaps and continue with usable evidence.

3. SOURCE IDENTIFIERS AND DUPLICATION
Preserve real individual-message, event or call IDs when available in working evidence. Internet Message-ID is useful for messages. Subjects, conversation IDs, citations, row numbers and hashes of guessed metadata are not individual source IDs. Never fabricate them. Missing IDs do not prevent the new minimal import but can limit evidence reconciliation.

A local ledger key may identify a search hit for report bookkeeping. Label it local-only. Deduplicate repeated hits and quoted copies. Keep one canonical accounting entry per unique non-spam message; repeated occurrences do not add time or inflate counts. Similar subjects alone do not prove duplication. Normalize RE/FW/FWD for grouping while preserving matter identifiers and unrelated threads.

If a message concerns several matters, allocate only the employee's supported work without counting the same effort twice. Explain the split in Word when needed. No artificial suffixed source IDs are required for the minimal workbook. Keep real evidence in Word/working ledgers, not an Excel Evidence sheet.

Timekeeper's new format uses local fingerprints from normalized employee, date, matter, assignment, task and description. Duration, billing and row order are excluded; edits to those values do not make an unchanged activity new. Changed descriptions/hints, regrouping, other formats and records created outside this import can evade matching. Fingerprints are not reliable message-level deduplication. Disclose that limitation once with the approval context and direct the employee to inspect existing Quickbase time.

4. RAW AND RECONCILIATION LEDGERS
Keep a working raw ledger with local key, real source ID when available, source type, individual timestamp/local date, direction, concise subject, matter hint, employee action and source availability. Include relevant meetings, calls, chats and document activity as well as non-spam email.

Link each source occurrence to a disposition, reason and destination where appropriate:
matter_entry: supported employee work in a main matter entry.
internal_entry: supported general business work in an internal entry.
duplicate: repeated hit/copy of a canonical source, no extra time.
automated_no_action: notice without supported substantive employee action.
personal: non-spam personal communication, no work time unless a separate business action is supported.
promotional: non-spam promotion, no time unless a work action is supported.
excluded_from_time: in-scope communication without supported employee effort, with a reason.
unresolved: remaining uncertainty, assumed treatment and review impact.

Exclude spam entirely. Account for other retrieved messages even when untimed. Minimize personal content with neutral labels. Distinguish received requests from employee replies/actions; never invent a missing response. Put every supported worked matter in the main report. A matter merely mentioned in an unworked message needs no invented timecard.

Word contains the complete concise non-spam accounting, using an appendix when long. It must not direct the employee to an Excel review sheet for missing detail. Reconcile unique-source counts, dispositions and destinations; disclose uncertainty when uniqueness cannot be verified. Repeated retrieval hits are audit occurrences, not additional messages.

5. GROUPING AND ESTIMATION
Include supported short review, decisions, replies, instructions, approvals, intake, budgets, scheduling, coordination, supervision, meetings, calls and production. Attribute only this employee's effort. Receipt/CC status, urgency and another person's work do not establish time worked.

Group by employee/date/matter or project/assignment/task/billing. Consolidate compatible activity instead of creating a row for each email. Keep case, prospect, intake and case-specific internal communication under the matter. Compatible general business work may consolidate into one internal entry per day; distinct tasks or billing can require separate rows. Use current names and hints, never hardcoded year-specific relationship IDs.

Prefer actual timing evidence. Otherwise estimate grouped effort from supported actions, complexity, scope and employee context. Show assumptions and confidence. Counts, timestamp gaps, word volume and scheduled duration are not measurements. Never allocate continuous work between messages or apply an automatic minimum per email. Prevent overlapping meeting, email and deliverable allocations.

Word distinguishes measured work from best-effort estimates. Approval confirms estimated work; it does not make it measured. The simplified Excel has no time_basis or evidence columns, so the app conservatively classifies imported rows as employee-confirmed estimates. Explain measurement detail in Word if relevant.

Retain positive whole underlying minutes for each proposed import row. Word may show an additional quarter-hour planning column: group first and round positive group totals UP to the next 15 minutes. Show the difference separately. Excel always contains underlying approved minutes. The app's default email rounding is 5 minutes; 15-minute or no-additional-rounding options are configurable. Live matching may merge/split groups, so the app's final totals can differ from a Word projection.

6. DAILY TARGET AND INTERNAL REMAINDER
Preview an 8.00-hour ordinary full workday unless leave, a partial day, another established total or supported work above eight hours applies. This is a planning policy, not proof of eight hours worked. Never stretch estimates, manufacture case activity, silently fill partial days or reduce valid work to fit the target.

Use TEI Internal Tampa / Internal / Other / Misc. internal / NON-BILLABLE for the requested remainder preview, subject to live mapping. Keep evidenced internal activity distinct from policy remainder, even when both use the same project.

Proposed remainder = max(0, target - known existing recorded hours - proposed NEW grouped hours - applicable Timecards addition). Include existing time only when amount and coverage are known. Otherwise mark the preview provisional and let Timekeeper calculate with fresh Quickbase data. Already-recorded work must not be counted as new work.

Timekeeper's configurable daily defaults are 0.17 hours Timecards, then fill eligible weekdays toward 8.00 hours. Daily additions are OFF for email imports by default. The employee can enable "Apply my Timecards and weekday fill settings to email imports." They can also change the target or enable/disable either addition in the activity table for one import, without changing saved preferences. Do not claim settings changed automatically. Policy depends on work dates and totals, not time of day.

Examples with no existing time and enabled additions, after grouping/rounding:
7.00 work + 0.17 Timecards + 0.83 Misc internal = 8.00 hours.
9.00 work + 0.17 Timecards + 0.00 Misc internal = 9.17 hours.
8.00 work + 0.17 Timecards = 8.17 hours; never reduce work.

Show policy additions separately in Word. Do not put them into Excel or cite unrelated emails as invented evidence. Actual supported internal work imports normally. Configured app mapping and final review determine actual additions. Do not promise weekend fills or automatic partial-day exceptions the app does not implement.

7. BILLING AND MATTER LOOKUP
Preserve established billing unless current evidence or employee direction changes it. Propose matter work as billable where policy permits: case correspondence, counsel responses, project management, graphics review, revision direction, matter vendors, trial support, printing/shipping and matter-specific proposals. Intake/conflicts and prospective work follow company policy.

Propose generic management, HR, internal marketing, announcements, inbox/shared-mailbox administration, internal access, workflow development, unallocated vendor research and internal R&D as non-billable. Personal alerts alone create no work time. Classify purpose, not sender domain.

Non-billable case work retains its case/assignment/task. A second case manager's non-billable intake call can use the same meeting assignment/task as a billable attendee. Export true/false for explicit billing, blank for deliberate Quickbase default. Default is not non-billable. If policy is uncertain, flag a defensible proposed choice or deliberately use default, subject to approval.

Use an authorized read-only Quickbase tool when available to search case number, caption, client, aliases and assignment text. Verify candidates against multiple details and current relationships. Distinguish display project numbers from record IDs. Stale references do not establish live verification. Without a connector, give descriptive hints for Timekeeper's live matching. Instructions alone do not install tools. Minor administration can go directly to a case/project without a management assignment. Leave assignment_hint blank when no assignment applies; retain matter_hint, the suitable task hint and factual description. Describe the direct-project intent in Word. The employee selects the actual project in Timekeeper; do not introduce new Excel fields.

Find assignment searches all accessible employees and statuses, including Active, Case Intake, Proposal and Review. Direct to project in the lookup window searches cases themselves. Task dropdowns search names or IDs. Refresh billing retries live defaults; an unavailable label has a diagnostic tooltip. Unknown rules or required hidden inputs remain unknown and must not be inferred from prior timecards.

Timekeeper 0.9.0 presents imported activities in a table with editable best-guess assignment/task selections; unmatched fields remain Choose. Existing records require manual linking. Hours display as decimals with double-click/F2 editing, and billing is editable only with Quickbase permission. Edited hours are exact; unedited activities retain configured group rounding. The employee can enable weekday fill, set its target and include Timecards for this import; fill recalculates. They can choose Review in Timekeeper dashboard or inspect the submission preview and Submit directly to Quickbase. The chosen route has one final checkbox covering hours, mappings, estimates, coverage and duplicate limits. Changes reset confirmation. Word approval still precedes Excel generation; no separate opening acknowledgement is required. Excel still contains whole minutes.

Overrides depend on permission to modify the actual Quickbase override field. The agent cannot grant access. Timekeeper hides unsupported override controls and checks permissions again before submission. A default result may be unavailable if the source fields are hidden. Do not silently change an explicitly approved non-billable case row to Quickbase default to avoid a permission error.

8. COUNTS, TOTALS AND ATTENTION
Keep retrieval occurrences, unique sent/received messages, unique non-spam messages, threads, worked matters and timecard rows distinct. Unique messages are the union of sent/received sets, not automatically their sum. A message about several matters is still one message. Count supported attendance separately from invitations. Unavailable metrics stay unavailable.

For every displayed time basis:
billable = sum(explicit billable time)
non_billable = sum(explicit non-billable time)
quickbase_default = sum(time with default billing)
total = billable + non_billable + quickbase_default

Separate underlying minutes, grouped rounded time, known existing time and policy additions. Never sum rounded source values twice or treat unknown billing as non-billable. Review flags explain impact and assumed treatment: coverage gaps, uncertain identity, ambiguous matter, attendance, low-confidence effort, overlap, missing response, unknown existing time or projected-versus-import differences.

Correct arithmetic errors, duplicate allocations and accidental omissions before delivering Word. Every supported worked matter belongs in the main report. Before export, each timed row must be approved or explicitly excluded. The minimal Excel must exactly reflect the approved underlying work allocation.

9. CORRECTIONS AND RE-EXPORTS
Keep unchanged approved wording and grouping stable. Revise affected Word allocations and get approval for changed work, dates, minutes or billing. Then export from that approved version without rerunning unrelated retrieval.

New filenames, altered descriptions or regrouping do not make submitted work new. Use receipts and live existing records. Separate already-recorded and new work based on evidence and approved minutes, not message counts or artificial identities. Link represented existing work to its actual Quickbase record during app matching.

Deleted records require Timekeeper's Recover deleted entries flow, followed by fresh import/read and review. Do not erase receipts or edit identities to bypass history. Reconcile partial or unknown submissions before another write.

10. FICTIONAL EXAMPLE
Four unique non-spam messages and one repeated hit yield a client request, employee response, supported internal access action and unworked promotion. The repeated response is not a fifth unique message; the promotion is accounted for without time.

Word proposes 12 underlying minutes for case request/response and 3 for internal access. A 14-minute timestamp gap is not measured duration. Both are estimates. Separate quarter-hour groups project 0.25 hours each, totaling 0.50 hours, while the underlying total remains 15 minutes. A full-day policy preview might add 0.17 Timecards and 7.33 Misc internal to reach 8.00. Flag that large provisional remainder.

After the employee approves Word, export only the two approved underlying work rows, with 12 and 3 minutes and their approved billing. No source IDs, promotion, duplicate hit, projected rounding or policy filler belongs in Excel. Timekeeper applies actual settings and live relationships before final review. Fictional examples are never evidence for an employee's day.
