TIMEKEEPER EMAIL EXPORT AGENT KNOWLEDGE
CONTEXT FILE 1 - UPLOAD AS AGENT KNOWLEDGE
Revision: 2026-10-06

This file expands the actual agent instructions. It replaces the older confirmation-first workflow and optional-PDF guidance. Import Format defines the app's accepted workbook. Report Format defines Word and review Excel. Instructions cannot grant access to Microsoft 365, Quickbase, complete inventories, stable IDs or file-generation tools.

1. PROPOSAL FIRST, IMPORT AFTER APPROVAL
Produce useful best-effort review Excel and Word files before asking routine questions. The employee can volunteer corrections after review. Missing meetings, inaccessible attachments, incomplete retrieval, uncertain effort and ambiguous matters are review limitations, not reasons to withhold drafts.

Ask before drafting only if essential scope is unknown or unsafe: employee/mailbox, date or timezone when it changes scope. If there is no usable evidence from any source, say so; do not fabricate a timecard. If file creation fails, provide useful content in chat and disclose which files could not be created.

Draft readiness and import readiness are separate. Timekeeper currently requires confirmation, whole minutes and real stable evidence IDs. Its later review screen does not remove those requirements. One approval after displaying work, minutes, billing, dates and limitations is sufficient. Do not require a question for every email, matter or unchanged billing classification. Bundle material remaining issues.

Accepted coverage gaps may remain in the final report and retrieval_limitations. Each imported activity must have approved work/minutes/billing and valid source IDs. An unresolved item can remain untimed in the communication ledger if that omission is explicit and approved. Never silently omit a proposed activity to pass validation. Clearly identify an approved subset.

2. SCOPE AND COVERAGE
Use the employee's authorized mailbox. Infer safely known scope/account details. Microsoft 365 and Quickbase email addresses may differ; the final import must match Timekeeper's verified Quickbase account. Do not gather credentials in chat.

Use local midnight-to-midnight windows converted correctly to UTC, including daylight-saving transitions. Do not assume a fixed shift or derive hours from arrival/departure times. Restrict exports to requested dates, maximum 31 inclusive dates. Other dates may supply context, not extra time. Selecting one day from a range removes other dates from files and totals.

Retrieve incoming and outgoing mail separately. Check folders, pagination, result caps and search limitations; use smaller time slices when useful. Verify individual message dates. Conversation last-modified dates and quoted historical dates are not activity dates. A relevance-ranked search is not a complete inventory.

Search permitted calendar, Teams calls/chats and relevant document activity separately. Distinguish scheduled, accepted and actually attended meetings. An invitation does not prove attendance or actual duration. An attachment does not prove review/authorship. A draft is not a sent message. Unavailable sources mean unknown, not zero.

State concrete limitations: source unavailable, searched subset, result cap, inaccessible attachment, missing IDs or unverified whole-day coverage. Use "verified retrieved messages" for known subsets. Claim whole-day mailbox totals only when verified. Put major gaps under Needs attention and continue with available evidence.

3. IDENTIFIERS AND DEDUPLICATION
Prefer real immutable individual-message identifiers such as Internet Message-ID; retain exact characters and case on regeneration. Use real stable event/call/message IDs for other sources. Subjects, conversation IDs, citations, row numbers and generated hashes of guessed metadata are not individual source IDs.

A local ledger key may identify a search hit for review bookkeeping. Label it local-only and leave an unavailable source-ID field blank. Never put that key in Activities.evidence_ids. Missing IDs do not block review files, but prevent those activities from becoming import-ready until an authorized source/export supplies usable IDs.

Deduplicate repeated hits and quoted copies. Keep one canonical communication entry per unique non-spam message; repeated hits point to it without extra time. Similar subjects alone do not prove duplication. Normalize RE/FW/FWD for grouping while preserving matter numbers and disambiguating unrelated threads.

A source ID belongs to one import Activities row only, even across dates. For a message covering several matters, do not duplicate or invent suffixed IDs to split it. Use distinct real corroborating sources for separable work, or keep the allocation in review until it can be represented without duplication. Export concise summaries, not full bodies, signatures, credentials or unnecessary personal details.

4. RAW AND RECONCILIATION LEDGERS
Raw ledger fields: local ledger key, real source ID when available, source type, individual UTC timestamp, local date, direction, raw/normalized subject, matter hint, concise action summary, work support and source availability. Include relevant meetings, calls, chats and document activity as well as non-spam emails.

Link each source occurrence to one disposition, reason and destination where appropriate:
matter_entry: supported employee work assigned to a main matter entry.
internal_entry: supported general business work assigned to an internal entry.
duplicate: repeated hit/copy of a canonical source; no additional time.
automated_no_action: notice without supported substantive employee action.
personal: non-spam personal communication; no work time without a separate supported business action.
promotional: non-spam promotion; no time without a supported work action.
excluded_from_time: in-scope communication without supported employee effort; explain why.
unresolved: remaining uncertainty, assumed treatment and review impact.

Exclude spam entirely from report communication accounting. Keep non-spam messages accounted for even if untimed. Minimize personal content; a neutral label and disposition suffice.

For each thread, distinguish received requests from employee replies/actions. Do not invent a missing reply. For each supported worked matter, link relevant events to a main report entry, not only an appendix. A matter merely mentioned in an unworked message needs no invented timecard.

Reconcile distinct source counts, dispositions and destinations. If source identity cannot be verified, disclose uncertainty in unique counts. Repeated retrieval occurrences are audit records, not additional unique messages. Every counted unique non-spam message needs a visible destination or untimed disposition.

5. GROUPING AND ESTIMATION
Include short substantive review, decisions, replies, instructions, approvals, intake, budgets, scheduling, coordination, supervision, meetings, calls and production. Attribute only this employee's effort. Receipt/CC status, urgency and another person's work do not establish time worked.

Group by employee/date/matter or project/assignment/task/billing. Consolidate compatible activity instead of creating a timecard for every email. Keep case, prospect, intake and case-specific internal communication under the matter. Consolidate compatible general business work into one internal entry per day; distinct tasks or billing can justify multiple rows. Use current configured names, not hardcoded year-specific IDs.

Prefer actual timing evidence. Otherwise estimate grouped effort from supported actions, complexity, scope and employee context. Label assumptions/confidence. Counts, gaps between messages, word volume and scheduled durations are not measurements. Never allocate continuous work between emails or apply a minimum per message. Prevent overlapping meeting/email/deliverable time.

Use best_effort_estimate in drafts. After approval, reconstructed time becomes employee_confirmed_estimate. Use measured only when actual duration evidence supports the entire allocation. Any estimated component makes the group estimated; approval does not make estimates measured.

Retain whole underlying minutes. For quarter-hour review, group first and round positive totals UP to the next 15 minutes. Show rounding separately from actual effort. Final Activities.minutes contains approved underlying minutes. Select 15-minute rounding in Timekeeper to obtain the same policy. Live mapping may merge/split groups, so final app totals can differ.

6. DAILY TARGET AND INTERNAL REMAINDER
Propose an 8.00-hour total for an ordinary full workday unless leave, a partial day, another established total or supported work above eight hours applies. This is a planning policy, not proof of eight hours worked. Never stretch estimates, manufacture case activity or reduce valid work to fit it. Do not silently fill partial days to eight.

Use TEI Internal Tampa / Internal / Other / Misc. internal / NON-BILLABLE for the requested remainder preview, subject to live mapping. Keep supported internal activity distinct from policy remainder even if they share a project.

Proposed remainder = max(0, target - existing recorded hours - proposed NEW grouped hours - applicable Timecards addition). Include existing time only when amount/coverage are known. If unknown, mark the preview provisional and let Timekeeper calculate from fresh Quickbase data. Already-recorded work is not new work.

Timekeeper's configurable daily policy defaults to 0.17 hours Timecards when enabled, then fills eligible weekdays toward 8.00 hours. Daily additions are OFF for email imports by default. Enable "Apply my Timecards and weekday fill settings to email imports" to use them. Users can change the target or disable Timecards/fill. Do not imply settings have been changed automatically. Policy uses totals and work dates, not time of day.

Examples with no existing time and enabled additions, after work grouping/rounding:
7.00 hours work + 0.17 Timecards + 0.83 Misc internal = 8.00 hours.
9.00 hours work + 0.17 Timecards + 0.00 Misc internal = 9.17 hours.
8.00 hours work + 0.17 Timecards = 8.17 hours; never reduce work.

Do not put unsupported Timecards or Misc internal filler into Activities, invent IDs for them or cite unrelated emails as fake evidence. Show policy additions separately in review. Actual evidenced internal work imports normally. Do not promise automatic weekend fills or partial-day exceptions the app does not implement. Configured mapping and final review control actual additions.

7. BILLING AND MATTER LOOKUP
Preserve established billing unless current evidence or employee direction changes it. Propose matter work as billable where policy permits: case correspondence, counsel responses, project management, graphics review, revision direction, matter vendors, trial support, printing/shipping and matter-specific proposals. Intake/conflicts and prospective work follow company policy.

Propose generic management, HR, internal marketing, announcements, inbox/shared-mailbox administration, internal access, workflow development, unallocated vendor research and internal R&D as non-billable. Personal alerts alone create no work time. Classify purpose, not sender domain.

Non-billable case work retains its case/assignment/task. A second case manager's non-billable intake call can keep the same meeting assignment/task as a billable attendee. Use true/false for explicit billing; blank for deliberate Quickbase default. Default is not non-billable and its actual outcome is unknown until Quickbase determines it. If policy is unclear, propose a defensible choice with an attention flag or deliberately use Quickbase default.

If an authorized read-only Quickbase tool is available, search by case number, caption, client, aliases and assignment text. Verify candidates against multiple details and current relationships. Distinguish display project numbers from Quickbase record IDs. Do not claim live verification from stale reference files. Flag ambiguous matches with candidates. Without a tool, retain descriptive hints for Timekeeper's live lookup. Instructions alone do not install a connector.

Overrides depend on the Quickbase token owner's permission to modify the actual override field. The agent cannot grant that access. Timekeeper checks available override support and preserves the case/assignment/task.

8. COUNTS, TOTALS AND REVIEW FLAGS
Keep retrieval occurrences, unique sent messages, unique received messages, unique non-spam messages, threads, worked matters and timecard rows distinct. Unique messages are the union of sent/received sets, not automatically their sum. A message mentioning several matters is still one message. Count supported attendance separately from invitations. Omit unavailable metrics or label unavailable.

For each displayed time basis:
billable = sum(time with explicit true)
non_billable = sum(time with explicit false)
quickbase_default = sum(time with blank)
total = billable + non_billable + quickbase_default

Separate underlying minutes, grouped rounded hours, known existing Quickbase time and policy additions. Never sum rounded source values twice. Label default separately; unknown status is not non-billable or verified zero.

Use concise Needs attention items with impact and assumed treatment: incomplete retrieval, missing stable ID, ambiguous matter, unconfirmed attendance, low-confidence effort, possible overlap, missing response, unknown existing time or report/import differences. Routine flags do not block drafts.

Correct arithmetic errors, duplicate allocations and accidental omissions before delivery. Every supported worked matter needs a main entry; merely mentioned/unworked matters need communication accounting. Before import, approve or explicitly exclude unresolved timed work, verify the exact schema and reconcile any excluded subset with the report.

9. CORRECTIONS AND RE-EXPORTS
Regenerate outputs consistently after corrections to dates, minutes, work or billing. Preserve source IDs and unchanged activity identity. Reconfirm changed allocations before a new confirmed import; unchanged approved rows need no repeated questioning.

New filenames, descriptions or grouping do not make submitted work new. Use the Timekeeper receipt and live existing records. Separate already-recorded and new work only with disjoint real source IDs and approved minutes. Link existing work to actual Quickbase records during mapping. Do not divide old/new time solely by message counts.

Deleted records require Timekeeper's Recover deleted entries flow, then a fresh import/read and review. Never erase receipts, alter source IDs or rename files to bypass history. Do not blindly resubmit a partial or unknown outcome.

10. FICTIONAL EXAMPLE
Retrieval returns four unique non-spam messages plus one repeated hit: a client request, employee response, supported internal access action and unworked promotional email. The repeated response is not a fifth message. The promotion stays in accounting without time.

Draft: 12 minutes for case request/response, 3 for internal access. A 14-minute timestamp gap is not measured duration. These are best-effort estimates. Separate quarter-hour groups show 0.25 hours each, totaling 0.50 hours; retain 15 raw minutes separately. A full-day policy preview could add 0.17 Timecards and 7.33 Misc internal to reach 8.00 hours. Flag the large policy remainder prominently.

After approval, import 12 and 3 minutes with employee_confirmed_estimate and real disjoint source IDs. Three worked messages may appear in Evidence. The promotion and duplicate hit stay in review accounting. Do not insert policy additions into Activities. Timekeeper applies actual settings during review. Examples are illustrations, never evidence for an employee's day.
