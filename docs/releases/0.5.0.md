# Timekeeper 0.5.0

## Email activity imports

Import the shared Microsoft 365 email agent's Excel workbook directly into Timekeeper. Employees confirm reconstructed activity and minutes in chat, then use **Import email Excel** to match each activity to current Quickbase assignments and tasks. Existing time can be linked instead of written again. A Toggl account is not required for this workflow.

Email time rounds after grouping by date, assignment, task and billing. Choose confirmed minutes, five-minute rounding (default) or fifteen-minute rounding in Settings. Automatic Timecards and weekday fill are off for email imports by default and can be enabled per user. Worked hours above the target are retained.

Workbooks are checked for account, timezone, dates, confirmed minutes, source coverage and plain values. Stable source IDs prevent repeated writes from regenerated files on the same installation. Existing Quickbase time, submission receipts, uncertain-result reconciliation and deleted-entry recovery remain part of review and submission.

## Billing in review

Every review row now offers **Quickbase default**, **Billable** and **Non-billable**, with gray, green and dim red indicators. Changing billing preserves assignment and task; email groups and totals recalculate when needed. Changes of date selection retain billing choices for retained rows.

Quickbase must expose the writable **Billable Override** field and grant Modify access through the user's roles. Timekeeper checks that access and confirms the calculated billing result after a write. A created record whose requested billing cannot be confirmed remains recorded in history, stops the submission, and must not be replayed. No permission bypass is included.

## Agent materials and installer

The email agent instructions and companion knowledge file include message reconciliation, sent/received distinctions, confirmed time estimates, optional PDF reporting and the exact Excel schema. The owner configures and shares the agent once. Microsoft 365 retrieval completeness and stable source identifiers must be verified in your tenant.

The installer includes version 0.5.0. Existing settings and history are retained. No live timecards were written during development or automated verification.
